Read the rhythm

Watch the enemy pull back and swing. Parry just before impact; dodge a little earlier.

Q Quickstep · W Flurry · E Onslaught
I Parry · O Dodge · P Heal

One defense attempt per hit. Late presses just after impact do not spend the next attempt; early follow-up presses are ignored. A brief flash on the enemy warns before each strike. Yellow attacks can be parried or dodged. Red attacks must be dodged. Parry an entire yellow combo to counter. A dodge blocks damage but forfeits the counter.

Quickstep: 2 strikes, no energy cost (+1 energy). Flurry: 3 strikes, 2 energy. Onslaught: 4 strikes, 3 energy. Each strike has its own random I/O/P prompt and resolves at weapon contact. A missed prompt lets the enemy evade that strike. The hero stays close until the combo ends.

P heals for 2 energy on your turn. During an attack, P is a timing input. On mobile, tap the matching icon in the bottom row.
